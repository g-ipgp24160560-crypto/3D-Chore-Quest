How to Play

Movement: Use W, A, S, D or the Arrow Keys on your keyboard to move the character. On a touchscreen, use the on-screen buttons.

Exploring: Walk up to chore stations (like a bed, sink, or garden). When you are close enough, an INTERACT button will appear.

Reading Challenges: Read the short story and answer the questions. Take your time! There is no time limit, and you can try again if you make a mistake.

Stars: Earn a star for every correct answer!
